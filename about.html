About Tool Dude

Why we built it

When our locksmith company needed a way to keep track of inventory, we found lots of options. However, most of them were overloaded with features we didn't need, seemingly to justify the cost of the product.

What we wanted was simply a searchable reference catalog that could also function as an inventory. And we wanted it offline. So it occurred to me that maybe, with help from AI, I could build this thing.

Our locksmith company now uses its own version of this app. It works, and the boss is happy. And I thought, why not expand it so that other trades could also use it?

How it works

The concept is straightforward. You have a standalone app that sits on your phone, and you download whichever trade data you want and load it into the app.

Keeping it going

The catch is that I pull together the trade data. It all needs to be updated regularly, in some trades quarterly if you want to be paranoid. This takes time, and sometimes the app needs updates to keep up with data changes. So I start to wonder: if this is valuable to people, isn't it fair that I get paid so I can live?

So let's see if it's valuable first. Then I might ask a small fee to cover living costs and internet service.

Until then, please comment, rate it, and feel free to make requests!
