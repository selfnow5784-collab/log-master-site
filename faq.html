FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about how Log Master works today.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After you open or install the app, it works without an internet connection. Your catalog stays on the device.

Where is my data stored?

On the device, in your browser. Log Master does not upload your catalog to this website or to the public code repository. If you clear the browser’s site data, or uninstall the app, that stored catalog can be removed, so keep a copy of your CSV file.

What CSV columns do I need?

For a catalog file to import directly, it currently needs these headers: Year(s), Make, Model, Product_Name, and id.

If those headers are not present, the app asks What is this file? You can then choose a file kind (Reference catalog, Inventory (in stock), or Vehicle ref), map your columns in the Import preview, and tap Import.

Is it free?

Yes. You can open, install, and use the app at no charge.

Can I use my own catalog?

Yes. That is how the app is meant to be used. On the Data tab, tap Import new CSV and choose your own file.
